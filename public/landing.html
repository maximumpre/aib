Important Fraud Update

  • We will never request that you hand over your card or pin to anyone or post your cards to an address.
  • We won't ask you to delete your mobile app or ask you to not log into your accounts for any period of time.
  • You should review your account regularly and if you cannot log in, contact us immediately.
  • Fraudsters are using text messages and phone calls pretending to be AIB staff.
  • Never provide login and personal information or security codes to anyone.
  • For more information visit our Security Centre.
